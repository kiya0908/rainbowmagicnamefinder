# Refund Policy

Last updated: August 03, 2026

This Refund Policy explains what happens when you use Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder, follow a link to another website, see an advertisement, or notice a charge that appears to use this site's name.

## 1. The Site Is Free

Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder's public features are free to use. This includes the name lookup tool, fairy catalog, book checklist, and reference pages. You do not need to buy a subscription, membership, download, credit package, or premium upgrade to use them.

The site does not currently sell or directly process payments for any product or service. It does not ask for payment-card details and does not create paid orders, recurring charges, or renewal obligations for visitors.

## 2. No Site Transaction Means No Site Refund

Because the site does not charge you, there is no Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder transaction to cancel, return, or refund. We cannot issue a refund for money we did not collect, and we cannot locate a transaction using bank or card details.

This section does not remove any consumer rights you may have against a third-party seller, advertiser, payment provider, or card issuer. Those rights and deadlines depend on the seller's terms and the law where you live.

## 3. Unrecognized or Suspicious Charges

Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder should not appear as the merchant for a card, bank, wallet, or app-store charge. If a statement contains an unfamiliar charge that appears to use this site, its domain, or a similar name:

1. **Contact your bank, card issuer, or payment provider promptly.** Use the official phone number, app, or website shown by that institution to report or dispute the charge. Applicable dispute deadlines can be short and vary by payment method and location.
2. **Secure the affected account.** Follow the institution's instructions about freezing the card, changing credentials, replacing the payment method, or reporting suspected fraud.
3. **Keep non-sensitive evidence.** Record the charge date, amount, currency, and complete merchant descriptor. If you take a screenshot, hide account numbers and unrelated transactions.
4. **Tell us about possible impersonation.** Email [support@rainbowmagicfairyname.online](mailto:support@rainbowmagicfairyname.online) with the non-sensitive details above and the page or message that connected the charge to this site, if known.

**Do not send us your full card number, bank account number, CVV or security code, PIN, password, one-time code, government ID, or payment-account login.** We do not need this information and cannot use it to investigate a charge.

We can review whether the site's name or domain may have been misused and can preserve relevant site records when reasonably available. We cannot access your bank or payment account, reverse a charge, guarantee recovery, or act as your financial institution.

## 4. Third-Party Retailers and Affiliate Links

Some pages contain links to independent third-party websites. For example, the book checklist includes clearly marked paid affiliate links to **Amazon.co.uk**. If you follow an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, this site may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you.

Opening a link does not create a purchase with Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder. Any order you place is a separate transaction between you and the third-party merchant. That merchant controls the price, payment, availability, delivery, cancellation, return, and refund process under its own terms and applicable law.

For an order placed with a third-party merchant:

- check the merchant name on your receipt or statement;
- contact the merchant through its official order or customer-service channel;
- follow the merchant's return and refund instructions; and
- contact your payment provider promptly if the merchant does not resolve a billing problem or if you did not authorize the charge.

We cannot view, change, cancel, return, or refund an order placed with Amazon.co.uk or another external merchant. Please do not send us an external order number unless our support team specifically asks for a non-sensitive reference.

## 5. Third-Party Advertisements

When advertising is enabled, the site may display ads delivered by third-party platforms such as **Google AdSense** or **Adsterra**. The site requests non-personalized ads from Google AdSense. An advertiser or destination merchant—not Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder—is the seller for a purchase made after you select an ad.

If an ad appears misleading, unsafe, inappropriate, or unrelated to the destination page:

- use the ad's own information or reporting control;
- for a Google ad, use **AdChoices**, **Why this ad?**, or Google's [Report an ad guidance](https://support.google.com/My-Ad-Center-Help/answer/13861201);
- contact the destination merchant about an order, cancellation, return, or refund; or
- email us a description, the page URL, and a redacted screenshot so we can review where the ad appeared.

We do not receive your card details from an advertiser and cannot issue a refund for an advertiser's or merchant's transaction. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) and [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie) for more information about third-party technologies.

## 6. Accidental Use and Cancellations

Using the finder, opening the catalog, checking a book off the checklist, or selecting a normal navigation link does not start a paid plan. There is currently no site subscription to cancel and no paid account balance, trial, or automatic renewal to manage.

If you intended to cancel something purchased elsewhere, identify the merchant on the receipt or statement and use that merchant's official cancellation process. Do not rely only on closing a browser tab, deleting a bookmark, or emailing this site.

## 7. Future Paid Features

If Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder introduces a paid feature in the future, the checkout will identify the seller or payment provider and show the price, currency, what is included, and any recurring-payment terms before payment. Product-specific cancellation, return, and refund terms will also be presented before purchase, and this policy will be updated before the paid feature becomes available.

Terms for a future paid feature will apply only to transactions made after that feature launches. They will not turn prior free use into a charge.

## 8. Policy Changes

We may revise this policy when the site's features, third-party relationships, or legal obligations change. The "Last updated" date identifies the current version. Material changes affecting a future purchase will be disclosed before the relevant transaction.
