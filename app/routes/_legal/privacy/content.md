# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

## 1. Who We Are

Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder is an independently operated fan-made name lookup available at **rainbowmagicfairyname.online**. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Rainbow Magic publishers, authors, illustrators, or other rights holders.

Questions and privacy requests can be sent to [support@rainbowmagicfairyname.online](mailto:support@rainbowmagicfairyname.online).

## 2. Information Processed by the Core Tool

The public name lookup runs in your browser against a catalog delivered with the site. We do not require an account or payment to use it. A name entered into the finder is used to calculate the result in the browser and is not intentionally sent to our server as a form submission.

Our hosting and security infrastructure may nevertheless process ordinary request data, including your IP address, requested URL, date and time, browser and device information, approximate region, referrer, and diagnostic or security logs.

If you email us, we process your email address, message, and any information you choose to include so that we can respond and keep an appropriate record of the request.

Book cover files are cached and served as **same-origin static assets** from this site's Cloudflare-hosted deployment. Merely viewing a cover therefore does not cause your browser to contact **orchardseriesbooks.co.uk**. Visible source-attribution links still point to that website; if you choose to open one, your browser will make a separate request to the destination host under its own privacy practices.

## 3. Analytics and Advertising Providers

Non-essential analytics and advertising scripts are disabled by default in the website configuration. If they are enabled after the required consent and account controls are in place, the site may use:

- **Google Analytics**, for audience and page-use measurement
- **Microsoft Clarity**, for aggregated interaction and usability measurement
- **Pageview**, for privacy-focused traffic measurement
- **Google AdSense**, for advertising delivery and measurement
- **Adsterra**, for advertising delivery and measurement

These providers may use cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons, IP address information, device or browser identifiers, viewed pages, ad interactions, and approximate location. They may receive data directly when your browser contacts their services. Their processing is governed by their own policies and by the consent choices available to you.

Google and its advertising partners may use advertising cookies to serve, personalize, limit, and measure ads. Where personalized advertising is permitted, ads may be based on prior visits to this or other websites. You can manage Google ad personalization through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) and learn how Google uses information from partner sites at [Google's partner-sites notice](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. Legal Bases and Consent

Depending on your location, we process necessary hosting and security data to provide the site, protect it, and pursue legitimate operational interests. We rely on consent before enabling non-essential analytics or personalized advertising where consent is legally required.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google advertising will remain disabled until an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform and required consent signals are configured. You may withdraw consent through the consent interface when it is available, or by clearing cookies and changing browser or provider settings.

## 5. Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information. We disclose information only to infrastructure, analytics, advertising, or support providers as described above; when required by law; or when necessary to protect users, the site, or legal rights.

## 6. Retention and Security

We retain support correspondence and operational logs only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, security, dispute handling, and legal obligations. Third-party providers set their own retention periods. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal data. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. To exercise a right, email [support@rainbowmagicfairyname.online](mailto:support@rainbowmagicfairyname.online). We may need enough information to verify and locate the request.

Browser controls can block or delete cookies. Google advertising choices are available in [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). Blocking non-essential technologies should not prevent use of the core public name lookup.

## 8. Children

The site is a general-audience fan reference and does not knowingly request dates of birth, school details, contact details, or account registration from children. A fairy theme alone does not establish the legal audience classification. We are reviewing the appropriate child-directed and age-restricted advertising treatment; advertising remains disabled by default while that decision and the required platform settings are incomplete.

If you believe a child sent personal information by email or another channel, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 9. Updates

We may update this policy when the site, providers, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.
