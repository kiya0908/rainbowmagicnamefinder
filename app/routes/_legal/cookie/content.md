# Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

## 1. What This Policy Covers

This policy explains how Rainbow Magic Fairy Name Finder may use cookies and similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, tags, and web beacons.

## 2. Current Default

The core name lookup does not need advertising or analytics cookies. Non-essential analytics and advertising integrations are disabled by default in the website configuration. Necessary hosting or security systems may still use short-lived identifiers or process an IP address to deliver and protect the site.

## 3. Technologies That May Be Enabled

After the required consent and platform controls are configured, the following providers may set or read cookies and similar identifiers:

- **Google Analytics** — site audience and usage measurement
- **Microsoft Clarity** — aggregated interaction and usability measurement
- **Pageview** — traffic measurement
- **Google AdSense** — ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, personalization where permitted, and measurement
- **Adsterra** — ad delivery and measurement

Provider cookies may be first-party or third-party and may be combined with IP address, browser, device, viewed-page, referrer, approximate-location, or ad-interaction data. Exact cookie names and lifetimes can change when providers update their services.

## 4. Your Choices

Where required, a consent interface will allow you to accept or reject non-essential purposes before related scripts run. For Google advertising in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, this requires a Google-certified consent management platform. Until that setup is complete, advertising remains disabled by default.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser. You can manage Google ad personalization through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). Rejecting non-essential cookies should not prevent the public name lookup from working.

## 5. More Information

See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for data categories, providers, purposes, rights, and contact details. Questions can be sent to [support@rainbowmagicfairyname.online](mailto:support@rainbowmagicfairyname.online).
